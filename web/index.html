vui in the browser

vui and vio built with Emscripten, drawn through WebGPU (WEB_PLAN.md).

  • Counters and plots: buttons, text shaped by HarfBuzz, live plots, a heatmap
  • Widgets: checkboxes, combo boxes, buttons
  • Cells: a terminal-style grid with bold and italic faces, colours and decorations
  • Chips: 200,000 movable, editable chips; drag to pan, wheel to zoom, drag a chip to move it, click one and type

Needs WebGPU: a current Chrome or Edge. Serve this folder over HTTP; file:// does not load the .wasm.
